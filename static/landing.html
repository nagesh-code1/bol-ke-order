How it works

From voice note to packed order in four steps

  1. Customer speaks

    A voice note in their own language, mixed words and all. Or they tap “same as usual”.

  2. Slip is ready

    Items matched to your rate list. Vague words like “woh wala tel” use their last order.

  3. You approve

    Unclear item? Out of stock? You choose, swap or ask. Then one tap: Accept.

  4. They hear back

    A voice reply in their language with the total. Udhaar goes straight into the khata.

How we calculate this

We assume a voice order takes about 3 minutes to replay, write down and price by hand, and about 1 minute to approve in Bol Ke Order, over 30 days. We assume 1 in 20 phone orders gets missed on busy days today. The value of a recovered order is your average order value (sales ÷ orders, capped at ₹600) × your margin. FAQ

Questions dukaandars ask us

Do I need a laptop or computer?

No. It runs in the browser on any Android phone. Your customers don't install anything either: they just send a voice note.

Does it work without internet?

It needs mobile data (4G is enough) to understand voice notes and send voice replies. A normal voice note takes a few seconds to turn into an order slip.

Can the AI change my prices or send messages on its own?

No. Prices always come from your own rate list. The AI only reads what the customer said. Nothing reaches a customer until you tap “Ask customer” or “Accept & reply”.

Which languages does it understand?

Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada, Tamil and English are tested today, including mixed sentences and self-corrections like “do nahi, teen kar do”. Telugu, Marathi and Bengali are next.

Is my shop data safe?

Your orders, customers and khata stay in your shop's own database. We never sell your data, and we never ask for bank details or OTPs.
